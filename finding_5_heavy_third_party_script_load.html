FixAudit all third-party tags and remove any duplicated analytics or marketing pixels. Defer non-critical scripts so they load after the main content, and prioritize essential scripts to improve perceived performance. This reduces the initial load burden and speeds up interaction readiness, leading to a smoother user experience and higher conversion rates.
